Sinigang na hipon

Description:

  • 1 lb Shrimp (cleaned, head-on for flavor)
  • 6 cups Water
  • 2 Medium tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 Onion, quartered
  • 1 cup Daikon radish, sliced
  • 6 Okra, trimmed
  • 1 Eggplant, sliced
  • 1 bunch Kangkong (water spinach)
  • 2 tbsp Fish sauce
  • 1 pack Sinigang mix or fresh tamarind
  • 2-3 long greens

Step:

  1. Prepare brothBring water to a boil in a large pot. Add onion, tomatoes, and radish. Cook for 8 minutes.
  2. Add shrimpDrop in cleaned shrimp. Cook for 1–2 minutes until they turn pink.
  3. Season soupStir in sinigang mix or tamarind extract. Simmer for 3 minutes.
  4. Add vegetablesPut in okra, eggplant, and long green chilies. Cook for 5 minutes until tender.
  5. Finish with greensAdd kangkong stalks first, then leaves. Season with fish sauce and ground black pepper.
  6. ServeTransfer to bowls and serve hot with steamed rice.
